You said it plainly: your productivity, and your income, is capped not by how fast you read images but by the menial work around each read — organizing screens, hunting down priors, re-typing boilerplate, hand-entering data into calculators. What we heard

Your time goes to the wrong tasks

  • Screen setup, every case. Splitting panes, loading images, applying presets — repeated by hand, and it doesn't persist between studies or workstations.
  • Chasing priors. Relevant prior studies and reports aren't pulled up automatically, so comparison means manual hunting.
  • Data entry you shouldn't be doing. Pulling structured values into calculators like the MESA calcium score, then transcribing results back into the report by hand.
  • Boilerplate, retyped. The same standard language, re-entered instead of pre-filled.

The bottleneck is the pre-cognitive work, not the interpretation. That's exactly what a tool should take off your plate.

What we propose

Meet RadAssist

RadAssist is a local, privacy-preserving AI assistant that automates the pre-cognitive tasks in your reading workflow. It runs quietly alongside your existing Exa PACS and dictation, on your own hardware, and learns how you work.

The line we will not cross

RadAssist never reads, interprets, or diagnoses images. It accelerates your workflow; you remain the sole diagnostic authority. That boundary protects your license, your patients, and your professional judgment.

A

A native screen recording — most important

The phone videos were perfect for understanding your day, but too low-res to read the actual fields. A native screen capture (recorded on the workstation, not filmed) of three loops: the calcium-score flow, template pre-fill / report creation, and hanging-protocol setup. On Windows, Win+G or the Snipping Tool records the screen — a few minutes each. Please use de-identified cases where you can.

Including the letterhead/format your final reports use, so the pre-fill matches your real output. Please remove all patient identifiers first.

F

Your top 3 highest-volume exam types

So we build the pre-fill where it saves you the most time first.
