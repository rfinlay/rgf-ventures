### A Privacy-Preserving Workflow Assistant for Radiology

## 1. What we heard

Your productivity, and therefore your income, is capped not by how fast you read images but by the **menial, repetitive work around each read**: organizing screens, hunting down priors, re-typing the same boilerplate, and hand-entering data into calculators. You put it plainly: the bottleneck is the **pre-cognitive** tasks, not the interpretation.

That single insight is the foundation of this proposal. Every minute of that overhead we remove converts directly into throughput, accuracy, and revenue.

## 2. What we propose: RadAssist

**RadAssist** is a **local, privacy-preserving AI assistant** that automates the pre-cognitive tasks in your reading workflow. It runs quietly alongside your existing Exa PACS and dictation, on your own hardware, and learns how *you* work.

**The line we will not cross:** RadAssist **never reads, interprets, or diagnoses images.** It accelerates your workflow; you remain the sole diagnostic authority. That boundary protects your license, your patients, and your professional judgment, and it keeps this tool clear of regulatory territory it has no business in.

## 3. The pilot (offered at no cost)

We propose to prove the concept with the exact task you already know is worth automating: the one you used to pay your son to do.

**Pilot scope: automated calcium-score reporting + report pre-fill.**
- Extract the structured data from the study/report
- Run the calcium-score calculation (MESA percentile)
- Insert the result and the matching guideline statement back into your report
- Pre-fill the report template with the correct boilerplate for the exam type
- Draft an end-of-report impression block for your review and sign-off

**Why this first:** it is high-value, self-contained, and does not require us to solve deep PACS integration to demonstrate real time savings. It is the fastest path to a result you can feel.

**This pilot is provided at no cost to you.** Our goal is to prove the value on your real workflow. Commercial terms come later, only if it earns them.

## 4. Our commitments to you

- **Privacy first, local by default.** Patient data is processed **on your machine**. Any AI model call is made through private, tokenized sessions with patient information minimized beforehand. Your data and your work product stay yours.
- **Non-intrusive.** RadAssist runs alongside Exa and your dictation without changing them. If you dislike it, you dismiss it instantly and nothing about your current setup is altered.
- **You stay in control.** Every automated entry is presented for your review. The tool proposes; you decide. Nothing is finalized or signed without you.
- **No diagnostic role, ever.** Workflow acceleration only.

## 5. What we need from you to begin

To build a pilot that fits your real workflow rather than a generic guess, we need the following from you. Most of this is quick, and we will work around your reading schedule.

**A. A native screen recording of your workflow (most important).**
The phone videos you shared were perfect for understanding the shape of your day, but they are too low-resolution for us to read the actual on-screen fields and menus. A **native screen capture** (recorded on the workstation itself, not filmed with a phone) of these three loops would let us build precisely:
1. **The calcium-score loop** — from opening a coronary CTA through entering data, getting the percentile, and putting it into the report.
2. **Template pre-fill / report creation** — how you load a template and start a report for a common exam type.
3. **Hanging-protocol setup** — how you arrange panes/presets when a study opens.
*(On Windows, the built-in Xbox Game Bar (Win+G) or the Snipping Tool's screen-record both work. A few minutes each is plenty. Please use non-sensitive or de-identified cases where possible.)*

**B. A technical point of contact in your IT group** and a short **discovery call** to map the Exa PACS integration surface (what it exposes, how you access it).

**C. Read-only inspection access** to a workstation (or a supervised session) so we can see the environment without disrupting your reading.

**D. The MESA Calcium Score Calculator details** you use, the exact web address and the input fields it expects.

**E. Sample de-identified reports and your report template(s)**, including the letterhead/format your final reports use, so the pre-fill matches your real output. **Please remove all patient identifiers before sharing.**

**F. Your top 3 highest-volume exam types** so we build the pre-fill where it saves you the most time first.

**G. Any compliance requirements on your side** (for example, a Business Associate Agreement or specific data-handling rules) so we meet them from day one.

**H. (Optional, only if you want the productivity dashboard later)** how your per-study compensation is structured, so a future phase can track output and revenue and reconcile your monthly statements.
