## Overview
The consultations center on a radiologist’s daily workflow challenges and the desire to automate manual, repetitive tasks to increase productivity, improve accuracy, and reduce cognitive load. <mark style="background-color: #F9EDD4">The customer, a practicing radiologist with over twenty-five years of experience, operates across multiple modalities (X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI, cardiovascular) using an "Exa" PACS suite on Windows-based workstations via Chrome.</mark> Key issues include excessive manual setup ("hanging protocols"), unstable formatting persistence across studies, lack of automated retrieval of prior studies, suboptimal voice recognition, time-consuming critical findings communication, repetitive data entry for clinical calculators (e.g., calcium score), and a lack of real-time productivity and revenue tracking. The customer seeks an AI-driven assistant to streamline pre-reading setup, automate report pre-filling and guideline generation, provide real-time dashboards, and reconcile monthly study counts, all while preserving data privacy and clinician autonomy. The proposed solution involves a local, privacy-preserving AI agent architecture that integrates with existing systems to automate pre-cognitive tasks and support business operations for independent contractors.
## Background
- The customer is a practicing radiologist, working as an independent contractor (ten-ninety-nine), with extensive experience across numerous PACS vendors and voice recognition systems. Compensation is productivity-based, creating a strong incentive for workflow automation.
- The workflow environment is Windows-based, accessing the "Exa" PACS suite via Chrome. The radiologist reads across modalities, with cardiovascular studies being the most lucrative, and uses custom templates for reporting.
- The radiologist frequently compares current studies with prior exams and reports, emphasizing the need for quick, automated access to historical data for cases like pituitary microadenoma follow-ups.
- Historically, the customer saved significant time by having his son prefill report templates, highlighting the value of offloading non-cognitive tasks.
- A developer is proposing a solution built on a local AI agent architecture (e.g., on a Mac Mini) using tokenized API calls to services like Claude and ChatGPT to ensure data privacy and local ownership of work product.
- Clinical urgency is a key factor, as demonstrated by a real-time scenario involving a pulmonary embolism, which required immediate initiation of critical findings communication protocols.
- There is a noted tension between the need for individualized, high-quality interpretation (aligned with AHA guidelines) and a corporate push for standardized report formats and surveillance tools (e.g., "Mosaic" app with eye/muscle tracking).
## Pain Points
- **Excessive Manual Pre-Reading Setup and Screen Organization (“Hanging Protocols”):** The radiologist must repeatedly perform manual steps to split screens, load images, arrange visualization panes, and apply presets before beginning diagnostic work. This repetitive setup does not persist between cases or workstations. **Impact:** Time-consuming clicks reduce productivity and delay diagnostic focus, directly affecting income.
- **Lack of Automated Retrieval and Side-by-Side Display of Prior Studies/Reports:** The system does not automatically fetch and display relevant prior reports or studies alongside the current exam, forcing manual searches. **Impact:** Delays comparison reporting and increases the risk of missing interval changes, reducing both efficiency and quality of care.
- **Repetitive, Non-Cognitive Data Entry and Manual Calculations:** Significant time is spent extracting structured data (e.g., age, sex, ethnicity, vessel scores) from reports to manually input into external calculators (e.g., Mesacalcium Score Calculator) and then transcribing the results back into the report. **Impact:** Time loss, risk of transcription errors, and reduced capacity for high-cognition interpretation.
- **Voice Recognition Instability and Risk of Report Corruption:** The voice recognition system is sensitive to ambient noise, which can corrupt reports if the microphone is active. Initial dictations are often slow due to sensitivity calibration issues. **Impact:** Report errors, rework, and interruptions decrease throughput and increase frustration.
- **Time-Critical Communication Burdens:** Reporting urgent findings, like a massive pulmonary embolism, involves manual initiation of "Critical findings, radiology communication protocol," which is a high-pressure task that distracts from interpretation. **Impact:** Elevates risk to patient outcomes if communication is delayed and increases cognitive load and liability concerns.
- **Lack of Real-Time Visibility into Productivity and Revenue:** As an independent contractor, the customer lacks a real-time dashboard to track daily study queues, completed counts, and revenue. This makes it difficult to optimize workload and verify monthly payments from contracting companies. **Impact:** Limited operational awareness, potential for payment inaccuracies, and time wasted on manual reconciliation.
- **Forced Standardization and Workflow Friction:** Medical corporations are pushing for uniform report formats and invasive surveillance tools ("Mosaic"), which conflict with the radiologist’s preferred, guideline-aligned interpretation style and raise privacy concerns. **Impact:** Loss of professional autonomy, potential compromise in report quality, and increased stress due to surveillance.
- **Data Privacy and Ownership Concerns with Cloud AI:** Using cloud-based AI platforms directly for report generation poses compliance and liability risks regarding data ownership and PHI. **Impact:** Potential for data breaches and loss of control over proprietary work product.
## Expectations
- **Automated Workflow Orchestration and Layout Persistence:** An AI assistant that automatically sets up the workspace for each exam type by preloading images, applying modality-specific presets, arranging screen layouts, and ensuring these preferences persist across workstations.
- **Automated Prior Study/Report Discovery and Comparison:** A system that automatically detects and displays relevant prior studies and their corresponding reports side-by-side with the current exam to streamline comparison workflows.
- **Automated Data Extraction and Calculator Integration:** The tool should automate the parsing of structured data from reports, auto-fill external calculators (e.g., Mesacalcium Score), and insert the calculated results (e.g., percentiles, guideline statements) directly into the final report.
- **Real-Time Productivity and Financial Dashboard:** A dashboard providing a real-time view of the study queue, completed counts, and daily/monthly revenue, along with an automated monthly reconciliation report to validate payments from contracting companies.
- **Robust and Secure Voice Recognition:** An improved dictation experience with better noise suppression, safeguards to prevent accidental dictation (e.g., mic-state locks), and reliable command invocation for templates.
- **Privacy-Preserving AI Assistant for Workflow Acceleration:** A multitask AI assistant that operates locally to learn user preferences and automate repetitive tasks, focusing on workflow acceleration rather than cognitive diagnosis, while ensuring all data remains private and secure.
- **Broad Compatibility and Minimal Disruption:** The solution must be non-intrusive, operating alongside existing PACS and voice recognition systems without causing workflow disruptions, potentially through a separate window or browser extension.
- **Market Scalability:** A successful pilot should lead to a commercializable product that can be offered to larger radiology groups on a fee-per-service or monthly support basis.

## AI Suggestions
- **Implement User-Profile-Based Layout Persistence:** Create a system (e.g., browser extension) that detects the modality/exam type and automatically applies saved screen splits, tool placements, and presets from a user profile.
- **Automate Prior Study and Report Retrieval:** Build a service that queries the PACS for relevant prior studies upon case opening and displays them side-by-side, with configurable matching rules.
- **Automate Data Extraction for Calculators:** Implement a document parsing pipeline to extract structured fields (age, sex, scores) from reports, auto-fill calculators like the Mesacalcium Score Calculator, and insert the results back into the report template.
- **Introduce Voice Recognition Safeguards:** Enhance voice recognition with adaptive noise gating, clear visual mic-state alerts, and confirmation prompts for commands to prevent unintended dictation and report corruption.
- **Build a Real-Time Productivity Dashboard:** Display key metrics like queue size, completed studies, and revenue in real-time. Add automated monthly reconciliation to track and verify payments against contracting company reports.
- **Develop a Privacy-First Workflow AI Assistant:** Create a local agent that learns user behaviors (layouts, presets, common steps) and applies them automatically, operating non-intrusively while keeping all data private and secure on the user’s machine.
