One order through prior authorization

An MRI order arrives at 9:00 on a Tuesday. Here is what the agent does with it.

  1. The order comes in from the referring office and lands in your RIS.
  2. The agent checks the patient's coverage and whether this payer requires authorization for this study.
  3. If it does, the agent assembles the request from the order and the clinical notes, and checks it against that payer's rules.

  5. The agent watches the payer's decision clock and puts the approval on the schedule the moment it arrives.
  6. If it is denied, the agent drafts the appeal with the reasons and puts it in front of a person. Nothing goes out unsigned.
