What an AI agent is, and how it works in your imaging center.

Two kinds of agent

Workflow agent. Works the queue in the background, inside your systems: a prior authorization, a cancelled slot filled from the waitlist, a records request. It works through your RIS and the payers' own electronic connections, not by clicking around a screen. A person on your staff approves every submission before it leaves the building.

Owner's agent. Text it or call it; it answers from your own numbers, never from memory. Every Monday it sends a short report in two views, operations and business health, and all three of you see both.

The guardrails

  • A person signs anything clinical and anything outbound.
  • Numbers are calculated and checked, never guessed.
  • HIPAA business associate agreements with every vendor that touches patient data.
  • Patient data stays in the United States.

One order through prior authorization

  1. The order arrives from the referring office and lands in your RIS.
  2. The agent checks coverage and whether this payer requires authorization for this study.
  3. If it does, the agent assembles the request from the order and clinical notes and checks it against the payer's rules.
  4. Your staff member reviews and approves. Only then does it go to the payer, electronically.
  5. The agent watches the payer's decision clock and schedules the study when approval arrives.
  6. If denied, it drafts the appeal for a person to review and sign.

You will click through this yourself: a working prior authorization demo on synthetic data, no real patients, already built. The production version is wired to your RIS and payers in Phase 4. rfinlay.github.io/imaging-priorauth-demo
