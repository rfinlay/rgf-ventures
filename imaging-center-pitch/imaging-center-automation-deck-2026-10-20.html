Boundaries: the FDA question, answered by the statute
Software intended "for administrative support of a health care facility, including the processing and maintenance of financial records, claims or billing information, appointment schedules, business analytics, information about patient populations, admissions, practice and inventory management, ... determination of health benefit eligibility, population health management, and laboratory workflow" is not a medical device.

Every candidate on the scorecard is on that list by name. The agents stop at the clinical boundary: nothing interprets an image, selects a protocol, or recommends a study.

Source: 21 U.S.C. 360j(o)(1)(A), read verbatim at Cornell Legal Information Institute. 360j(o)(2) further provides that FDA "shall not regulate" excluded administrative functions even when they coexist with device functions.
