The free move

Put four things in every vendor contract before you sign. They cost nothing today.

  • API access to your own data, named in the contract, not "available on request."
  • HL7 interface count and fee schedule fixed at signature, not quoted per interface later.
  • Data export rights in standard formats, on demand, at no charge.
  • DICOM query permissions (worklist, query/retrieve, DICOMweb) for systems you authorize.

Before signature these are contract terms. After signature they are hostage terms.

Before the numbers: what an AI agent is

Software that does a job inside your systems, and asks a person before anything leaves the building.

One that runs a workflow

An operations agent works one process from start to finish: a prior authorization, a cancelled slot filled from the waitlist, a records request. It works through your RIS and the payers' own electronic connections, not by clicking around a screen. Someone on your staff approves anything that goes out.

One you talk to

An owner's agent you text or call. Ask how many MRIs were read last week, or which referrer went quiet, and it answers from your own numbers. Every Monday it sends a short report: volumes by modality, reader, and referrer; authorization and denial rates; reconciliation.

One order through prior authorization

An MRI order arrives at 9:00 on a Tuesday. Here is what the agent does with it.

  1. The order comes in from the referring office and lands in your RIS.
  2. The agent checks the patient's coverage and whether this payer requires authorization for this study.
  3. If it does, the agent assembles the request from the order and the clinical notes, and checks it against that payer's rules.
  4. Your staff member reviews it and approves. Only then does it go to the payer, over the payer's electronic connection.
  5. The agent watches the payer's decision clock and puts the approval on the schedule the moment it arrives.
  6. If it is denied, the agent drafts the appeal with the reasons and puts it in front of a person. Nothing goes out unsigned.
