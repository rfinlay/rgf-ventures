What I am proposing

A design engagement over your six-month build. $30,000 total, two phases, with a gate you control.

  • Phase 1, $12,000, first 90 days. Contract language for the four access terms. RIS, PACS, billing, and voice vendor evaluation. Everything that has to happen before you sign a vendor.
  • Phase 2, $18,000, back 90 days. Written specifications for prior auth, records, backfill, and owner reporting, plus payer-specific prior auth design. You own it.
  • At 90 days you decide whether Phase 2 goes. Build priced from the specification after you open, with real volumes in hand.

What you own if I disappear

  • The specification, written for an engineer who has never met me.
  • The code, in a repository under your organization's account.
  • Runbooks for every operational procedure and vendor contact.
  • Standard interfaces only: HL7 v2, DICOM, payer APIs, a named model vendor under its own BAA.
  • Your RIS and voice vendor contracts in your name, not routed through me.

What I recommend you do not buy from me

  • A custom voice agent. Buy a vendor.
  • Reminders and intake. Use the RIS.
  • A platform. This is built for this center and operated by me.
  • Anything that touches the image clinically.
