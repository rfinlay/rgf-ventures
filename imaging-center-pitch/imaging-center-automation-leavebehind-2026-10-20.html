Summary of a conversation, El Paso, 2026-10-20

Six months of free decisions.

Prepared for Dr. Luis Ramos and Fernando Escarzaga
by Richard Finlay, RGF Global Ventures LLC

A center projected to open around April 2027, with equipment being signed, real estate being toured, no staff hired, and no systems chosen. Every operational automation decision is cheap and reversible today and will not be in six months.

The free move, whatever you decide about me. Put four terms in every vendor contract before signing: named API access to your own data, HL7 interface count and fee schedule fixed at signature, data export rights in standard formats at no charge, and DICOM query permissions for systems you authorize. Before signature these are contract terms. After signature they are hostage terms.

What I am proposing

A design engagement over your six-month build. $30,000 total, two phases, with a gate you control.

  • Phase 1, $12,000, first 90 days. Contract language for the four access terms. RIS, PACS, billing, and voice vendor evaluation. Everything that has to happen before you sign a vendor.
  • Phase 2, $18,000, back 90 days. Written specifications for prior auth, records, backfill, and owner reporting, plus payer-specific prior auth design. You own it.
  • At 90 days you decide whether Phase 2 goes. Build priced from the specification after you open, with real volumes in hand.

What you own if I disappear

  • The specification, written for an engineer who has never met me.
  • The code specific to this center, in a repository under your organization's account. Existing product of mine that I extend for you, under a perpetual license with the source in your hands.
  • Runbooks for every operational procedure and vendor contact.
  • Standard interfaces only: HL7 v2, DICOM, payer APIs, a named model vendor under its own BAA.
  • Your RIS and voice vendor contracts in your name, not routed through me.

What I recommend you do not buy from me

  • A custom voice agent. Buy a vendor.
  • Reminders and intake. Use the RIS.
  • Anything built to resell to other centers. This center is the reference implementation and the priority; your service terms do not change if I ever take on a second site.
  • Anything that touches the image clinically.
