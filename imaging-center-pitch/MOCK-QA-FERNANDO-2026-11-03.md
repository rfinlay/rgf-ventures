## Q3. "$45k before a single patient. Why now, not after opening?"
GIO-IMPROVED (7/10): "You're not spending forty-five thousand now. You're spending seven, for Phase 1, and then you decide. Here's why now: the cheapest moment to get your data terms right is before you sign with a RIS or PACS vendor. Data export, interface fees, API access. Once the contract is signed, you've lost that leverage, and fixing it later means renegotiating or rebuilding. Waiting until you're open doesn't save money. It means paying to redo decisions you could have made for free."

## Q5 (Jesse). "Where does patient data live, who can see it, including the AI company?"
GIO-IMPROVED (4/10; law requires US, not Texas): "Three parts. Where: in the United States, always. Texas law has required that for patient records since January. Every vendor we pick has to show it in writing, and so does anything I build. Who: only the people whose job needs it. Access is by role, every view and change is logged, and your center sits in its own isolated environment, with its own database and keys. No other organization can see it. The AI company: only under a signed business associate agreement. That makes them legally bound under HIPAA, they don't train on your data, and the AI only sees what a task needs. Anything clinical still gets a human signature before it leaves the building."
