Subject: Offshore billing and Texas SB 1188, the answer

Luis, Fernando, Jesse,

Fernando asked on Thursday whether an offshore billing team in India would conflict with Texas SB 1188. The short memo is attached. It is research for your counsel, not legal advice.

The short version: SB 1188 controls where patient records are physically stored, not who can access them. If the records stay on US servers and the billing team works through a locked-down remote session with no downloads, that appears permissible under SB 1188 itself.

The real risk is Medicaid. The Texas Medicaid managed care contract bars offshore remote access, and your MCO provider agreements may pass that rule down to you. Until your attorney reads those agreements, Medicaid billing should stay with US staff.

The memo lists the safeguards to write into any billing contract and six questions for your attorney. The billing-audit agent would also watch the vendor every week: charges against reports and payments, and the vendor's access logs.

Happy to walk your attorney through it.
